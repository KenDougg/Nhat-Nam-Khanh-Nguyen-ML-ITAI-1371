📌 About This Course

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the fundamental concepts, algorithms, and applications of Machine Learning. Throughout the semester, I am exploring how to build, evaluate, and deploy models that can learn from data to make predictions or decisions.

My Interests in ML
I am particularly interested in the intersection of Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing, specifically focusing on how Vision-Language Models (like CLIP and BLIP) can be fine-tuned for specialized tasks. I also enjoy the challenge of optimizing model performance through hyperparameter tuning and exploring the mathematical foundations of unsupervised learning.
