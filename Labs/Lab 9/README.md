What is the main idea behind ensemble methods?

The main idea is the "wisdom of the crowd"—combining the predictions of multiple models to create a more accurate and stable final result. A "committee" is often better because individual models (like a single Decision Tree) can be prone to "overfitting" or making errors on specific details.
